## Welcome to the PhoenixSuite Foundry!

We are an open source and community driven modding organization.

The PhoenixSuite of mods (essentials, excavate, chronicles, guilds, solaris, and domains) as well as the modpack Phoenix Forge Technologies are included.

### Our mission.
We want to have fun making mods and modpacks! We have a focus on making projects with pretty UIs, good documentation, good dx/ux, and an open community towards new ideas. If we make mistakes we will own up to them and learn+improve in the modded community!

### Included Projects
- Phoenix Forge Technologies: Our flagship modpack. All current projects came to being because Phoenixvine wanted a specialized mod for PFT.
- PhoenixCore: The Core-mod for PFT where it provides a backbone for everything unique to PFT.
- Phoenix Chronicles: Our QuestBook mod designed to fit the PhoenixSuite's vibe but also boasts good UX/DX. It is currently in beta due to how complex it is
It is also our biggest standalone mod. Chronicles is the mod with the most need for testing/development so any support is hugely appreciated!
- Phoenix guilds: Our Teams mod with an easy to use UI, a custom flags system, and extensive API for other devs to use. It is mostly a backbone mod.
- PhoenixWiki: The PhoenixSuite's library mod. It handles theming, the rich text engine for our mods, the ingame wiki system, and the suite hud buttons.
- Solaris: Our map mod. It is visually light with vibrant water, theming, the ability to restrict certain parts of the mod through it's api, and has built in support for FTBTeams and PhoenixGuilds.
- Phoenix Domains: Our chunkloading/claiming mod. It supports Journeymap, Solaris, Phoenix Guilds, and FTBTeams.
- Phoenix Essentials: Our essential commands mod. It has support for Phoenix Domains to not /rtp into claimed land.
- Phoenix Excavate: Our vein mining/placing mod. It has an ingame UI to choose what blocks it mines, the amount you can vein interact with at one time, and some settings. The config file can lock down the control ingame provides.
- Phoenix Archives: Our lore codex mod. It shares the rich text chronicles has (that Wiki provides) as well as the ability to jump to quests from lore entries.
It also has conditions to unlock entries for packdevs.
- Phoenix's Chromatic Codes: Our custom effects mod. It piggybacks off of base mc formatting codes so the mod should work with most text. It supports a bunch of unique movement effects and also named codes (&[name]).
- Phantasia: Our 2d/3d tutorials mod. It is similar to Create's Ponder but works without create and is fully authorable ingame with editors.
- Phoenix Tesla Network: Our GregTech:Modern addon for wireless EU, wireless chargers, and a custom armor set named the PhoenixTech Suite with controllable flight.
- Phoenix's Fission: Our GregTech:Modern fission addon. It has an extensive set of heat models for packdevs, 3 built in reactor types, and kubejs definable fission component blocks.
- Phoenix Gregic Additions: A small GregTech:Modern addon adding a more powerful HPCA.

### Where to go from here

We also have an online wiki here [Online Wiki](https://p-h-o-e-n-i-x-packforge.github.io/PhoenixSuite/wiki/) with the github repo for it named PhoenixSuite if you want to contribute to it!

If you have any ideas, suggestions, or issues feel free to tell us in our github issues or dropping a ping to Phoenixvine on discord.
